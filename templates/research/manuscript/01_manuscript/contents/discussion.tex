\section{Discussion}

The support-vector machine outperformed logistic regression on this small
training subset, as expected for a non-linear classifier on raw pixels.
The more useful outcome of this example is the record behind the numbers:
each registered claim resolves to a recorded run, its inputs, and the
downloaded data file with its published checksum. If any of these changes,
\texttt{clew verify} fails and the live paper marks the affected claims and
the node where the evidence broke.

Numbers that are not registered, such as the dataset sizes typed into the
Methods, are not checked. Registering a number is what makes it checkable.
