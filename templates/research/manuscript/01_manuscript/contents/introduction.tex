\section{Introduction}

This short paper is the worked example shipped with the SciTeX research
template. It is not a contribution to digit recognition: MNIST and the two
classifiers compared here are long established~\citep{lecun1998gradient,cortes1995support}.
Its purpose is to show one small analysis carried from the literature,
through data, analysis, statistics and figures, to claims that a reader
can check: every result number in this paper is linked to the file and
the computation that produced it, and the live paper shows which of those
links still hold.
