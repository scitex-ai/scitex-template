\section{Results}

Both classifiers were trained on the same \vclaim{n_train} training images
and evaluated on the official test set (Figure~\ref{fig:01_mnist_overview}).
Logistic regression reached a test accuracy of \vclaim{logreg_accuracy},
and the RBF support-vector machine reached \vclaim{svc_accuracy}.

The two classifiers disagreed in correctness on a minority of test images:
\vclaim{only_svc_correct} images were classified correctly only by the
support-vector machine, and \vclaim{only_logreg_correct} only by logistic
regression. McNemar's test on these paired outcomes rejected equal error
rates ($\chi^2 = $~\vclaim{mcnemar_chi2}, $p < 0.001$).
