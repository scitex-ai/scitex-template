\section*{Data Availability Statement}

MNIST is publicly available (CC BY-SA 3.0, Y.~LeCun and C.~Cortes); the
exact file used is identified by its SHA-256 checksum, recorded by
scitex-dataset. The code, configuration and manuscript are in this project;
\texttt{make all} regenerates every result, figure and claim.
