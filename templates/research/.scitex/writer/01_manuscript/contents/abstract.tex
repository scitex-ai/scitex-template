\begin{abstract}
  \pdfbookmark[1]{Abstract}{abstract}

We compare logistic regression with an RBF support-vector machine on a
subset of the MNIST handwritten digits, as the worked example of the SciTeX
research template. The data are fetched with a verified checksum, every
analysis step records its inputs and outputs, and every result number in
the text is a registered claim linked to the computation that produced it.
The accompanying live paper shows, for each claim, whether that link still
holds and, when it does not, where it broke.

\vspace{1em}
\end{abstract}
